MEDICAL THRILLER / DENNIS ROSS

The Lethal Elixir

A novel by Dennis Ross

A deadly infection. A global conspiracy. A race against time.

When Alex Williamson, a quiet laboratory employee at Deaconess Hospital, falls ill with a highly contagious and lethal infection, the hospital is thrown into panic.

Infectious diseases specialist Maggie Hamilton discovers that Alex has been infected with a weapons-grade strain of Ebola. With the potential for a worldwide epidemic, Maggie and a newfound FBI ally must travel across the country to find the perpetrators before the elixir is unleashed upon the world.
